## Why Telography?

Running a news channel takes hours every day: reading sources, rewriting stories, finding images and posting on time. Telography does all of it for you, so you can focus on growing your audience.

## How it works

1. **Collect** — Telography gathers the latest stories in the categories you choose.
2. **Write** — AI rewrites each story in a clean, channel-ready style and translates it into your language.
3. **Illustrate** — every post gets an AI-generated cover image.
4. **Review** — you approve, edit or skip each draft with one tap in Telegram.
5. **Publish** — approved posts go live on your channel, or publish automatically on your schedule.

Everything happens inside Telegram. There's no dashboard to learn.

## Features

- 📰 **News from many categories**, always fresh
- ✍️ **AI rewriting and translation** into your channel's language
- 🖼️ **AI cover images**, with one-tap regeneration
- ✅ **You stay in control** with approve, edit and skip buttons
- ⏰ **Scheduled auto-publishing** in your own timezone
- 💬 **24/7 AI support** right inside Telegram
